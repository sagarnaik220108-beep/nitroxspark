## 🧐 What is Sentinel AI?

**Sentinel AI** is an intelligent security companion built for anyone who uses AI to create apps, websites, and digital tools.

You don't need to know how to code. You don't need to understand cybersecurity. You just give Sentinel AI your project, and it instantly makes sure your creation is safe, locked down, and ready for the world.

---

## 💥 The Big Problem: "Built in a Weekend, Hacked on Monday"

Today, anyone can build a software application in a single afternoon. You open an AI tool, describe what you want in plain words:
> *"Build me a food delivery website where people can log in and pay with credit cards."*

The AI writes hundreds of lines of complex code in 10 seconds. You run it, and it works! It feels like magic. People call this **"vibe coding."**

### But there’s a dangerous hidden catch:
AI tools are trained to make things **look like they work**, not to make them **safe**.
* 🚨 **AI leaves the front door unlocked:** It often writes code with passwords and secret keys pasted right in the open where anyone can see them.
* 🚨 **Nobody reads the code:** Because the AI wrote it in seconds, the creator doesn’t read it line-by-line. They don’t even know what to look for.
* 🚨 **Real people get hurt:** Studies found that nearly **half of all AI-written code has severe security flaws**. In 2025, over 170 brand-new AI-built apps accidentally leaked real users' home addresses and payment credentials to the public on day one.

In short: **Millions of everyday creators are publishing apps with hidden traps they didn't create and don't know how to fix.**

## 💡 How Sentinel AI Solves This (In Simple Words)

Sentinel AI acts like an expert cybersecurity bodyguard standing right next to you. It takes the fear and confusion out of cybersecurity through 4 simple steps:

### 1. 🔍 Instant Health Check
The moment you connect your project, Sentinel AI checks every corner in less than a second. It searches for all the common mistakes AI makes—like accidentally writing your secret password inside the app or leaving your user database wide open.

### 2. 🗣️ The "Human English" Translator
Cybersecurity is usually full of scary, confusing words like *"CWE-89 SQL Injection Sink"*.  
Sentinel AI translates that into plain everyday English:
> *"A stranger can type a trick text into your login box and get inside your admin dashboard without typing a password."*

### 3. 🎬 Safe Attack Simulator (Show, Don't Tell)
Most people only learn their app was broken *after* they get hacked.  
Sentinel AI shows you a safe, animated simulation of how a hacker would try to break in—and proves how your app will block them once it's fixed.

### 4. 🪄 One-Click Magic Fix
You don't need to hire an expensive consultant. You simply click **"Auto-Fix"**.  
Sentinel AI replaces the dangerous parts with safe, bulletproof code automatically.

## 🎯 The Breakthrough: An "Honest Scorecard"

Most online scanners play psychological tricks: if you click "Scan" 10 times, they bump up your score just to make you feel good.

Sentinel AI introduces the **Honest Scorecard (0 to 100)**:
* **Real & Deterministic:** If your code has 3 dangerous bugs, your score stays at 54 whether you click once or 10 times.
* **No False Hope:** Your score only goes up when your code is *actually* safer.
* **Score 100 means true peace of mind:** You know with 100% certainty that your users and your data are protected.
